# Oarside harbour records

The Oarside Harbour Board kept yearly records of the cargo landed and shipped across its quays. The
figures below are total tonnage handled, inward and outward together.

| year | tonnage handled |
|---|---|
| 1880 | 64,000 t |
| 1890 | 212,000 t |
| 1900 | 351,000 t |
| 1910 | 486,000 t |

The jump after 1883 followed the opening of the railway to the harbour. Slate from the Fallowmere
quarry became the largest single outward cargo after the branch opened in 1896, followed by woollen
cloth from the Brennick mills. The main inward cargo was coal.

From 1884 the harbour master's office was in the Oarside Quay station building, which the cooperative
let to the Harbour Board for a nominal rent.

A storm in the winter of 1904 damaged the outer breakwater; the 1900 to 1910 growth was slower than
it might have been because the eastern quay was closed for repairs for most of 1905.
