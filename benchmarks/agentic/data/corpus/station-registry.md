# Station registry

Stations of the Lume Valley Rail Cooperative, in order from Brennick down the line. Distances are
kilometre posts measured from Brennick Central (see glossary-and-units). Kestrel Wood and Lume Bridge
Halt are on the main line between Holm Cross and Oarside; Fallowmere is on the upland branch, which
leaves the main line at Holm Cross.

| code | station | town or place | opened | platforms | km post |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| BRN | Brennick Central | Brennick | 1877 | 4 | 0.0 |
| HXC | Holm Cross Junction | Holm Cross | 1877 | 3 | 31.4 |
| KSW | Kestrel Wood | Kestrel Wood | 1899 | 1 | 52.8 |
| LMB | Lume Bridge Halt | Lume Bridge | 1916 | 1 | 55.1 |
| OAT | Oarside Town | Oarside | 1885 | 2 | 70.6 |
| OAR | Oarside Quay | Oarside | 1883 | 2 | 72.0 |
| FLM | Fallowmere | Fallowmere | 1896 | 1 | 31.4 + 24.9 on the branch |

Notes:

- Oarside Quay was the terminus from 1883. Oarside Town, on the hill, was added in 1885 after
  complaints that passengers had to climb from the harbour.
- Lume Bridge Halt opened after the viaduct was rebuilt (see bridge-survey-1911).
- Holm Cross Junction gained its third platform when the Fallowmere branch opened.
