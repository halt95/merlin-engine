# The 1902 timetable

The timetable that came into force on 1 May 1902 was the first to run expresses over the whole line.

Main line, Brennick Central to Oarside Quay:

- Express: 1 h 52 min, calling at Holm Cross Junction and Oarside Town only. Seven a day in each
  direction.
- Stopping train: 2 h 40 min, calling at every station. Five a day in each direction.

Fallowmere branch, Holm Cross Junction to Fallowmere: 58 min, four passenger trains a day in each
direction, plus slate trains as required.

Expresses were worked by Class C engines. On Sundays only two expresses ran each way.

For comparison, the 1925 timetable, worked by Class K engines over the rebuilt viaduct, cut the
express to 1 h 38 min and added a call at Kestrel Wood. Do not confuse the two timetables.
