# The Fallowmere slate quarry

The Fallowmere Slate Company works the quarry on the western face of Fallowmere Fells. Slate has been
cut there since the eighteenth century, but output was small until the railway branch opened in 1896.
Before then slate went down the valley by cart to Holm Cross, a journey of most of a day.

| year | output | workforce |
|---|---|---|
| 1890 | 9,500 t | 210 |
| 1900 | 38,000 t | 640 |
| 1910 | 52,000 t | 710 |

The company signed a 21-year carriage contract with the cooperative in 1895, a year before the branch
opened, guaranteeing a minimum of 20,000 t a year. On the climb a Class F engine could haul at most
six loaded wagons; slate trains coming down the branch ran with up to fourteen wagons and extra brake
vans.

Most of the slate was shipped from Oarside harbour (see harbour-records).
