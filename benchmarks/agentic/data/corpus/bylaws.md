# Bylaws of the cooperative

The cooperative is governed by a general meeting of members and a board of nine directors elected by
the members.

**Voting.** Each member has one vote, however many shares they hold.

**Quorum.** As adopted in 1874, a general meeting was quorate when one-fifth of the members were
present. An amendment passed in 1901 raised the quorum to one-quarter of the members, because
meetings in the harvest months had been passing motions with very few members present.

**Chair.** The board elects the chair each year from among the directors. There was originally no
limit on re-election. The 1908 amendment limits a chair to 12 consecutive years in office.

**Dividends.** Profits after maintenance are paid to members in proportion to the shares they hold,
capped at six per cent of the share value a year; any surplus goes to the renewal fund.

**Amendments.** A bylaw may be changed only by a two-thirds majority of the votes cast at a quorate
general meeting.
