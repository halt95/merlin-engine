# Glossary and units

**Down** means towards Oarside and the sea; **up** means towards Brennick. A "down express" leaves
Brennick.

**Kilometre posts** are measured from the buffer stops at Brennick Central along the main line. On the
Fallowmere branch, posts restart at zero at Holm Cross Junction, so a branch location is written as
the junction's post plus the branch distance.

**Ruling gradient** is the steepest sustained gradient on a line, written as 1 in N: the line rises
1 m for every N m travelled. A smaller N is steeper.

**Tonnage** in the harbour records means metric tonnes (t) of cargo handled, inward and outward
together.

**Crown** was the valley's unit of currency before decimalisation; a share in the cooperative cost
five crowns.

**Halt** is a small station with no staff, where trains stop only on request.
