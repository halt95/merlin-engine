# Rolling stock

The cooperative used four classes of steam locomotive before 1920.

**Class A "Heron"** (1876). Six built by an outside firm for the opening of the line. Top speed
56 km/h. Withdrawn by 1912.

**Class C "Curlew"** (1889). Eleven built, also by an outside firm. Top speed 72 km/h. The Curlews ran
the expresses until 1908.

**Class F "Fellrunner"** (1895). Four tank engines built for the Fallowmere branch, which climbs at
a ruling gradient of 1 in 38. Top speed 45 km/h. The gradient dispute that delayed the branch was
settled only when the Fellrunner design showed that a short, heavy tank engine could climb it with
six loaded slate wagons.

**Class K "Kingfisher"** (1908). Nine built, the first engines built by the cooperative itself, at
its works in Holm Cross. Top speed 88 km/h. The Kingfishers took over the expresses from the Curlews.

There was no Class B, D or E: the letters were reserved for designs that were never ordered.

The engine works at Holm Cross opened in 1886. Before then, engines were repaired in a shed at
Brennick Central.
